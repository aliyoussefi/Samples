# Human Resources persona pack

This persona pack shows how Microsoft Scout can act as an always-on HR chief of staff. The goal is to make HR personas more productive by watching employee, manager, recruiting, policy, meeting, and task signals, then turning scattered HR work into prioritized, approval-ready follow-through.

---

## Value proposition

Human Resources teams lose time when operational signals, approvals, conversations, files, and follow-ups are scattered across many systems. Scout helps by acting as a user-facing chief of staff that:

- surfaces manager, employee, candidate, and policy signals that need attention now
- prepares users for employee relations, workforce planning, and interview debrief meetings
- tracks promised follow-ups, stale candidate actions, and unresolved manager asks
- drafts manager updates, candidate communications, intake notes, and meeting briefs
- keeps the human in control before anything is sent, recorded, or updated

Scout is productivity support for staying prepared, responsive, and focused on the work that matters most.

---

## Common human resources systems and work surfaces

These samples are designed to fit teams that use tools such as:

- Communications: Outlook, Teams, calendar, meeting transcripts
- HR systems: Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM, Microsoft Viva, ServiceNow HRSD
- Recruiting systems: LinkedIn Talent Solutions, Greenhouse, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, Workday Recruiting
- Planning and notes: OneNote, Planner, SharePoint, OneDrive, Power BI

The prompt should only use systems the user is authorized to access.

## Persona 1: HR Business Partner

**Job to be done:** Support managers, prepare for people discussions, track employee relations follow-ups, and keep workforce actions moving.

**Scout value:** Scout watches manager messages, employee-related follow-ups, calendar events, HR case signals, policy asks, and planning artifacts for time-sensitive issues, missing inputs, and unresolved commitments. It drafts manager updates and prep briefs for review.

## Persona 2: Talent Acquisition Lead

**Job to be done:** Keep hiring pipelines moving by coordinating candidates, interview loops, feedback, and hiring manager follow-through.

**Scout value:** Scout watches candidate communications, hiring manager threads, interview schedules, recruiting systems, feedback loops, and unresolved hiring commitments for stale actions, missing feedback, and candidate responsiveness gaps. It drafts updates and coordination notes for review.
