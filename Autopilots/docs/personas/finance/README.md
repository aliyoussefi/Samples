# Finance persona pack

This persona pack shows how Microsoft Scout can act as an always-on finance chief of staff. The goal is to make finance personas more productive by watching approvals, forecasts, budget signals, vendor conversations, meetings, files, and unresolved commitments, then turning scattered finance work into prioritized, approval-ready follow-through.

---

## Value proposition

Finance teams lose time when operational signals, approvals, conversations, files, and follow-ups are scattered across many systems. Scout helps by acting as a user-facing chief of staff that:

- surfaces budget, forecast, approval, and vendor signals that need attention now
- prepares users for forecast reviews, budget meetings, and procurement decisions
- tracks promised follow-ups, stale approvals, and missing inputs
- drafts stakeholder updates, variance notes, supplier follow-ups, and approval summaries
- keeps the human in control before anything is sent, approved, uploaded, or updated

Scout is productivity support for staying prepared, responsive, and focused on the work that matters most.

---

## Common finance systems and work surfaces

These samples are designed to fit teams that use tools such as:

- Communications: Outlook, Teams, calendar, meeting transcripts
- Finance systems: Dynamics 365 Finance, SAP, Oracle, Workday Adaptive Planning, Anaplan
- Procurement and spend: Coupa, Ariba, Icertis, Zip, ServiceNow procurement workflows
- Planning and reporting: Excel, Power BI, OneNote, SharePoint, OneDrive

The prompt should only use systems the user is authorized to access.

## Persona 1: FP&A Manager

**Job to be done:** Prepare forecasts, explain variance, track budget risks, and keep leadership reporting current.

**Scout value:** Scout watches Outlook, Teams, calendar, forecast files, Power BI, budget trackers, and unresolved finance asks for variance questions, missing inputs, forecast risks, and leadership reporting needs. It drafts concise updates and forecast prep notes for review.

## Persona 2: Procurement Manager

**Job to be done:** Keep supplier decisions, sourcing events, purchase approvals, and contract handoffs moving.

**Scout value:** Scout watches vendor conversations, purchase requests, approvals, meetings, procurement systems, and contract handoffs for missing inputs, stalled approvals, expiring terms, and supplier follow-ups. It drafts stakeholder and supplier updates for review.
